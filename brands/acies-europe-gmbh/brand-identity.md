# Brand Identity: Acies Europe GmbH

**Client:** Acies Europe GmbH (engagement labelled "ACIES GmbH")
**Last updated:** 2026-04-13
**Status:** Working

> Curated working doc. Raw audit trail lives in `discussion-log.md`.

---

## Who They Are

Acies Europe GmbH is the fully-owned European subsidiary of the ACIES group, established to continue offering ACIES' insurance intermediation services within the EU post-Brexit. It is operationally the same business as ACIES MGU, but licensed and domiciled in Germany under §34d GewO.

**Phoenix Europe** is a registered trading name of Acies Europe GmbH.

This entity is not a separately-positioned brand. Its purpose on the web is regulatory: to evidence active, licensed German operation to supervisory authorities, counterparties, and anyone checking the public intermediary register.

---

## What They Stand For

Inherited from the ACIES group register (from MGU workshop): **modern, solid, trustworthy, with a refreshing outlook**. On this entity specifically, the emphasis leans toward **solid and trustworthy** — the page's job is institutional credibility, not attitude.

---

## What They Want to Put Out into the World

That ACIES has a real, licensed, professionally-run EU arm — not a nameplate. The page should read as **substantive**, not promotional.

---

## Origin Story

Post-Brexit, UK-licensed intermediaries lost passporting rights into the EU. Acies Europe GmbH was established in Hamburg so ACIES could continue placing business for European clients through a locally licensed entity. Phoenix Europe is operated as a trading name under this same licence.

---

## Target Audience

**Primary audience:**
1. German supervisory authorities & register-checkers (Handelskammer Hamburg, DIHK, BaFin-adjacent enquirers) verifying that ACIES' European operation is real and compliant.
2. European counterparties (carriers, brokers, capacity partners) doing KYC/diligence on Acies Europe GmbH before transacting.
3. Consumers or intermediaries who need Impressum / dispute-resolution information under §5 TMG and §36 VSBG.

**Secondary audience:** Existing ACIES clients who click a footer link from the main site to verify the European entity's status.

**What they currently believe / feel:**
Uncertain whether ACIES has a credible EU presence, or explicitly checking a compliance box. Not in buying mode.

**What problem this brand solves for them:**
Evidence of active, regulated German operation — findable, accurate, and legally compliant.

**Where they currently look for solutions:**
Public vermittlerregister.info, Handelsregister, the main ACIES site's legal-notices area (currently insufficient).

---

## Position Within Group (Multi-Brand)

**Relationship:** Fully-owned European subsidiary of ACIES group (parent: ACIES MGU Holdings).
**Sibling brands / MGAs:** Subscribe, ACIES SME UK, Phoenix, TED-mar, Invision.
**Trading name operated by this entity:** Phoenix Europe.

**Endorsement model:** Endorsed parent — the page should be visually and verbally consistent with the main ACIES brand so that the visitor understands this is ACIES' German arm, not a separate business.

---

## Key Differentiator

N/A at entity level — differentiation lives at the ACIES group level. The page's "differentiator" is simply that it exists, is accurate, and reads as credible.

---

## Brand Feeling

**Register for this page specifically:** Institutional, calm, precise, slightly formal. Avoid any marketing-flavoured language. Think "annual report page" or "investor relations disclosure", not "product marketing page".
