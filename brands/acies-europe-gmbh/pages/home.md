---

## Purpose

Evidence that ACIES has an active, licensed, German-regulated insurance intermediation operation. Satisfy §5 TMG (Impressum) and §§15–17 VersVermV disclosure obligations. Give supervisors, counterparties, and consumers a single authoritative URL they can check.

Not a marketing page. Not a product page. A regulatory evidence page.

---

## Primary Audience

1. German supervisory authorities and register checkers (Handelskammer Hamburg, DIHK, BaFin-adjacent enquirers).
2. European counterparties doing KYC on Acies Europe GmbH (carriers, brokers, capacity partners).
3. Consumers and intermediaries looking up dispute-resolution routes under §36 VSBG.
4. (Secondary) Existing ACIES clients arriving from a footer link on the main site.

---

## Key Messages

- Acies Europe GmbH is a real, licensed German insurance intermediary — §34d GewO, Reg. No. D-XVP1-R1NNR-84, supervised by Handelskammer Hamburg.
- It is the European arm of the ACIES group, established post-Brexit to continue serving European clients under German authorisation.
- Phoenix Europe is a trading name of Acies Europe GmbH.
- All required statutory disclosures (Impressum, remuneration, dispute resolution, privacy) are present and findable in one place, in both German and English.
