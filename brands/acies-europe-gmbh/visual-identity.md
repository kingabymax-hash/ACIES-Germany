## Guiding Principle

This is a regulatory-evidence page. Visual direction defaults to **endorsed parent** — it should read as the main ACIES brand's German arm, not as a separate design system. Keep the design quiet, institutional, and typographically led. No marketing ornamentation.

---

## Colors

**Primary color:** _TBD — inherit from main ACIES site (likely Oxford Blue or similar institutional dark)._
**Secondary color:** _TBD — inherit._
**Accent color(s):** Minimal. A single understated accent for links and anchor markers.
**Background:** White or very soft off-white. No dark mode requirement.
**Text color:** Dark grey / near-black for body; darker still for headlines. High contrast for legibility and accessibility compliance.

**Mood and intent:** Modern, solid, trustworthy. On this page, lean institutional over refreshing — the page's job is credibility.

**Colors to avoid (group-wide from Subscribe workshop):** Red, yellow, neon / fluorescence, loud or overly bright colors, brown.

**Notes:** Confirm once domain/hosting decision is made whether we use main ACIES palette verbatim or a slightly more muted variant for the regulatory context.

## Typography

**Heading font:** _TBD — inherit from main ACIES site if available; otherwise a clean serif or humanist sans (e.g. Inter, Source Sans, or an institutional serif like Source Serif / Tiempos)._
**Body font:** Same family as headings or a paired body sans for long-form legal text legibility.
**Special-use fonts:** None required.

**Heading character:** Confident, calm, no display-face flourishes.
**Body character:** Highly readable at small sizes — there is a lot of legal text on this page, so line-height, measure, and x-height matter more than personality.
**Weight & style preferences:** Regular + semibold for hierarchy. Avoid thin weights for long-form legal copy.
**References:** Annual report pages, investor relations microsites (e.g. Allianz IR, Munich Re regulatory pages).
**Notes:** Prefer system-safe or open-license fonts to avoid licensing headaches on a quasi-regulatory page.

## Imagery & Aesthetic

**Style:** Minimal to none. This is not a page that needs photography.
**Existing assets:** TBD — main ACIES logo / wordmark should be available; confirm whether a Europe GmbH lockup exists or needs to be created.

**Photography direction:** Likely none. If any imagery is used, a single quiet establishing image (e.g. Hamburg cityscape, or a neutral interior) rather than staged people photography.
**Illustration direction:** None.
**Icon style:** If any icons are used (e.g. for contact, download, dispute resolution links), very simple line icons, monochrome.
**Overall aesthetic:** Typographic, document-like, quiet.
**What to avoid:** Stock imagery of handshakes, glass office buildings at sunrise, diverse teams smiling at laptops. No hero video. No parallax. No carousels.

---

## Overall Visual Feel

A typographically-led institutional one-pager that looks like an extension of the main ACIES brand. Restrained, legible, credible. Closer to an annual report or regulatory disclosure page than a marketing site.
